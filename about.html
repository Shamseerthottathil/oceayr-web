About OCEAYR

Creative Thinking.

Designed to be desired.
Built to be remembered.

OCEAYR is a creative marketing agency focused on luxury hospitality, food and lifestyle brands. We help hotels, resorts, restaurants, cafés, wellness spaces and destination-led businesses communicate with greater clarity, desirability and commercial confidence.

Our work begins by understanding the guest, the brand promise and the experience being sold. We then translate that insight into refined strategy, distinctive visual storytelling, campaign direction and digital content that feels consistent from social media to website, from launch material to in-property communication.

We combine editorial taste with an organised production process. The result is work that looks premium, remains practical to manage and supports real business goals — stronger awareness, better engagement, more qualified enquiries and a brand people remember.
